            How this is calculated

            A project counts as needing risk intervention if someone has flagged it directly in ClickUp (Risk Status, or an explicit "at risk" health), it's simply fallen behind schedule and isn't done, or its next stage gate is within 14 days while its stage-gate checklist (Checklists tab) still isn't fully checked off. The first two come straight from ClickUp; the checklist signal is computed here in this browser, since the checklist itself is saved locally rather than in ClickUp. A project already flagged for another reason gets this reason added to it rather than a second row.
